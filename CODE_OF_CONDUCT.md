# AgentBox Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a respectful, harassment-free, technically rigorous community. Contributors of every background and experience level should be able to participate without intimidation or discrimination.

## Expected behavior

- discuss ideas and evidence, not personal worth;
- give actionable, proportionate feedback;
- acknowledge uncertainty and correct mistakes openly;
- protect private and security-sensitive information;
- respect project scope, safety gates, licensing, and third-party terms;
- stop harmful behavior when asked by maintainers.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate disclosure of private information, credential or exploit dumping, impersonation, sustained disruption, and retaliation are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report community conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not use a public Issue when that would expose a target or sensitive evidence. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close interactions, restrict participation, or ban accounts based on severity and pattern. Conflicts involving a maintainer should be documented and escalated to another future project maintainer when available.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is officially representing AgentBox elsewhere.
